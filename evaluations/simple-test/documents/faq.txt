Northwind Labs FAQ

Q: What are the office hours?
A: The office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. Remote work is allowed up to three days per week.

Q: How do I request IT support?
A: Email it-support@northwind.example or open a ticket in the internal helpdesk. Urgent issues are answered within one hour.

Q: Where can I find the expense form?
A: The expense form is available on the intranet under Finance. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days.
